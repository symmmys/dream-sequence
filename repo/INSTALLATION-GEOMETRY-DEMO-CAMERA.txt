CAMERA CONTROLS
Follow mode is the default. Press C or click FREE CAMERA (C) to enter inspection mode.

Free camera outside a corridor:
  W / S       Forward / backward
  A / D       Strafe
  R / F       Up / down
  Mouse drag  Look
  Wheel       Adjust speed
  Shift       Faster
  C           Return to following the player

Free camera inside a corridor:
  W / S       Along the corridor tangent
  A / D       First cross-section axis
  R / F       Second cross-section axis
  E / Q       Third cross-section axis
  Mouse drag  Look
  Wheel       Adjust speed
  Shift       Faster
  C           Return to following the player

The four corridor directions are the tangent plus the three transported perpendicular directions. The controls provide an independent forward/back pair for each direction: W/S, A/D, R/F, and E/Q respectively. The free camera can cross a corridor independently; the player remains stationary while the inspection camera moves. Switching back to Follow returns to the player.
