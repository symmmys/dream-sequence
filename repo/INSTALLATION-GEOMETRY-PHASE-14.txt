SOMNIUM — GEOMETRY PHASE 14
Main-world W traversal and Poincare camera safety

INSTALLATION

1. Extract this complete ZIP into a clean project directory.
2. Serve the directory containing index.html:

   cd /path/to/somnium-project-root
   python3 -m http.server 8081

3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8081/.
4. Phase 14 uses the cache-busting identifier:
   somnium-20261108-phase14-w-traversal-chart-boundary-1
   Hard-refresh after replacing an existing project directory.

EXPECTED BEHAVIOR

- On the Main World side of an anomaly, the character uses the faster Main
  World movement profile (180 walk / 360 run). Entering a chamber restores
  chamber-scale movement (4.5 walk / 7.5 run).
- E and Q both move along W while the player is within an anomaly's influence
  field. In the transition interior, the bounded region extends six ambient
  W units beyond each endpoint by default.
- The Poincare free camera remains inside the finite world chart while moving
  through an anomaly. Third-person-to-free-camera switching also constrains
  the inherited camera pose.
- Arrival into a Poincare chart constrains the final player/camera position
  before projection. Projection validation itself remains strict.
