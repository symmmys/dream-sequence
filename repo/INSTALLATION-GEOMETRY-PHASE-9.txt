SOMNIUM — GEOMETRY PHASE 9 INSTALLATION
Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-slice-cross-section-1

RECOMMENDED INSTALLATION
1. Back up your current Somnium directory.
2. Extract somnium_geometry_phase9_hypercube_slices_project.zip into a NEW directory.
3. Enter the extracted project directory containing index.html.
4. Start the local server:

   python3 -m http.server 8081 --bind 127.0.0.1

5. Open http://127.0.0.1:8081/.
6. Select 4D GEOMETRY DEMO from the main menu.
7. Confirm the console contains:

   [Main] Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-slice-cross-section-1
   [GeometryDemo] Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-slice-cross-section-1

CONTROLS
- WASD: player movement in the chamber.
- C: toggle follow/free camera.
- Free camera W/S, A/D, R/F: move through ordinary 3D space.
- Free camera E/Q: move the W slicing hyperplane through the tesseract.
- Mouse drag: look around the cross-section.
- Mouse wheel: adjust free-camera movement speed.
- Shift: move faster.
- G: toggle the geometry debug grid.

WHAT TO VERIFY
- The tesseract appears as a translucent 3D polyhedron with bright edges, not a ball of coincident markers.
- At initial W=0 the HUD should show approximately 12 vertices and 8 faces.
- Press C to enter free camera.
- Hold E or Q and observe both the polyhedron and the HUD vertex/face counts change.
- Move with W/A/S/D/R/F to inspect the section from different 3D positions.
- Far enough along W, the section should shrink and eventually disappear when the slice no longer intersects the tesseract.
- Existing corridor traversal and the Poincare/main-world connections should remain functional.

The geometry is computed from the 16 distinct R4 corners and the intersections of their cubical facets with the current W hyperplane. This is a genuine cross-section, not a perspective scaling effect.
