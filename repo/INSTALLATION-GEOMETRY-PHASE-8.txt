SOMNIUM — GEOMETRY PHASE 8 INSTALLATION
Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-visibility-short-corridors-1

RECOMMENDED INSTALLATION
1. Back up your current Somnium directory.
2. Extract somnium_geometry_phase8_hypercube_visibility_project.zip into a NEW, clean directory.
3. Enter the extracted project directory containing index.html.
4. Start the local server:

   python3 -m http.server 8081 --bind 127.0.0.1

5. Open http://127.0.0.1:8081/.
6. Select 4D GEOMETRY DEMO from the main menu.
7. Confirm the console contains:

   [Main] Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-visibility-short-corridors-1
   [GeometryDemo] Build: 2026-11-08-hypercube-visibility-short-corridors-1

CONTROLS
- WASD: player movement in the chamber.
- Shift: sprint.
- C: toggle follow/free camera.
- Free camera W/S: forward/back.
- Free camera A/D: lateral.
- Free camera R/F: vertical.
- Free camera E/Q: W coordinate.
- Mouse drag: look around in free camera.
- Mouse wheel: adjust free-camera speed.
- G: toggle geometry debug grid.

WHAT TO VERIFY
- The starting hypercube chamber shows a large cyan tesseract with thick glowing edges and 16 vertex markers.
- The follow camera frames the tesseract rather than looking down at the player.
- The tesseract changes shape continuously as it rotates in the XW plane.
- Press C and use E/Q to inspect the projection at different W viewpoints.
- The two anomaly corridors traverse in roughly four seconds at normal demo speed, depending on frame rate and input.
- Both corridor routes still connect the hypercube chamber, Poincare chamber, and main world.

The ZIP includes the previous project features. No migration is required for existing corridor descriptors: omitted intrinsicLengthScale defaults to 1.
