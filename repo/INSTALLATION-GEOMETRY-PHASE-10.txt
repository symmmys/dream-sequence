SOMNIUM — GEOMETRY PHASE 10 INSTALLATION
Build: 2026-11-08-slice-transition-anomaly-hotfix-1

1. Extract the project archive.
2. Open a terminal in the directory containing index.html.
3. Start the local server:

     python3 -m http.server 8000

4. Open this address in a desktop browser:

     http://localhost:8000

5. Open the 4D Geometry Demo from the main menu.

MOVEMENT

Ordinary world:
  WASD              Walk
  Mouse drag        Look around
  Space             Jump
  E/Q               No effect on W (fourth-axis movement is locked)

Inside a slice-transition anomaly:
  WASD              Move in the 3D slice plane
  R/F               Move along local Y
  E/Q               Move along the fourth axis / slice normal
  Arrow Left/Right  Turn
  Shift             Sprint

Free inspection camera:
  C                 Toggle follow/free camera
  WASD              Move
  R/F               Vertical movement
  E/Q               W movement only after entering a slice-transition anomaly
  Mouse drag        Look
  Mouse wheel       Adjust movement speed
  Shift             Move faster
  G                 Toggle metric grid

DEMO ROUTE

- The first connection links the 4D Hypercube Chamber at W=140 to the
  Poincare Chamber at W=0. Press Q to move toward decreasing W.
- The second connection links the Poincare Chamber at W=0 to the Somnium Main
  World at W=80. Press E to move toward increasing W.
- XYZ movement remains available inside either anomaly, within its bounds.
- Once the player crosses a connected destination slice plane, the player is
  transferred to that slice and ordinary 3D movement resumes.

The demo retains explicit topology and chunk-graph connections. W is not part
of a world chunk address; the anomaly's CorridorChunk tracks occupancy during
4D traversal.
